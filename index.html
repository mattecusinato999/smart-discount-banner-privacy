Privacy Policy for Smart Discount Banner

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Smart Discount Banner operates as an embedded Shopify application. To function correctly, we collect and process:

  • Shop Domain & Identification: Your Shopify store domain (e.g., myshop.myshopify.com) and internal installation IDs.
  • Configuration Data: Banner settings created within the app (custom text, background/text colors, discount codes, display parameters).

We do not collect or store personal identifiable information (PII) of your end-customers.

2. How We Use Information

Collected data is strictly used to store and display your customized discount banner on your storefront via Shopify Theme App Extensions / App Embed Blocks.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

When you uninstall Smart Discount Banner, all configuration settings associated with your account are automatically queued for deletion in accordance with Shopify’s mandatory webhooks (shop/redact).

4. Third-Party Services

The application infrastructure is securely hosted on Render and Supabase.
